# Task 7: Walk the schedule

Walked 2026-07-28 (server clock `Tue Jul 28 2026`, ET) against `localhost:3000` as
`dev.volunteer@yale.edu`, the Task 3 persona: cleared, on the SU26 VADM roster, with nine
published shift assignments across the term's Saturdays.

The fixture date matters for everything below. Today is 2026-07-28, so of the volunteer's nine
shifts **four are already in the past** (Jun 6, Jun 20, Jul 4, Jul 18) and five are ahead
(Aug 1, Aug 15, Aug 29, Sep 12, Sep 26). The term is roughly at its midpoint, which is the
ordinary state of a schedule for most of a semester. Most of what follows is the app having no
concept of "now" on this surface.

Surfaces walked: `/schedule` (My schedule, including the availability editor and both request
forms), `/schedule/full` (all 18 clinic dates, default and selected), and `/schedule/requests`.
Density claims are measured with `browser_evaluate` against the live DOM at a 1200x805 viewport,
not eyeballed; the measurements are quoted inline.

## Needs its own brainstorm

- **F-07-3, shift time and location.** The app has no field anywhere for when a clinic shift starts or where it happens, and adding one is a modelling decision rather than a rendering fix. The open questions: does time hang off `Term` (one set of clinic hours for the semester), off each entry in `Term.clinicDates` (which would need those to stop being a bare `DateTime[]`), off `Department` (departments plausibly start at different times), or off `ShiftAssignment` (maximum flexibility, maximum authoring burden)? Does location vary by department, given that some departments are remote and the schema already carries a `remote` tag per assignment? Is a single clinic address enough, or does the Food Pharmacy sit somewhere else? Whatever shape is chosen also has to reach the `schedule-reminders` cron email, the dashboard next-shift hero, `/schedule/full` and the AVS generator's assumptions, so the fan-out deserves a plan before the migration.

## Coverage notes

**What I could not walk.**

- **Real schedule density on `/schedule/full`.** The SU26 fixture schedules exactly one department (VADM) on every clinic date, so the densest real page I could load was one card with four people, 840px tall. F-07-9's production numbers therefore come from a DOM measurement of the real card cloned to 30, not from 30 real departments; the cloned card is also the minimum-size one, so the 4,263px figure understates a real clinic Saturday. The two-column resolution at 1200px, the 229px card height and the absence of any filter control are all measured on the shipped page, not extrapolated.
- **`/schedule/requests` as a volunteer.** The brief's step 3 asks for a request to be submitted here, but this route is the approver-side surface: it gates on `manageableRequestDepartmentIds` and redirects to `/no-access` when the list is empty (`src/app/(app)/schedule/requests/page.tsx:36-39`). As `dev.volunteer` I was redirected to `/no-access` and got the standard "You don't have access to that page" screen. The gating is correct and the route is not linked from the volunteer's schedule nav (which shows only "My schedule" and "Full schedule"), so there is no dead link to report. The volunteer's actual request flow lives in the `<details>` disclosure on each `/schedule` shift card, and that is where steps 3's findings (F-07-4 through F-07-6, F-07-10) were walked. Approver-side handling of the requests belongs to Task 9.
- **Multi-term and multi-department views.** `mySchedule` renders one section per term with headings once a member is on two (`src/app/(app)/schedule/page.tsx:253-258`), and the sidebar has plural "Roles"/"Departments" branches (`:498-513`). The persona is on one term in one department, so neither branch rendered and neither is covered here.
- **The availability editor.** It rendered (18 date chips grouped by month, "Based on your application responses.") and is in the same viewport as the shift list, but the brief scopes Task 7 to the three surfaces above, so I read it without walking a save. No availability finding is filed.
- **Director-side effects.** I confirmed by reading `approveRequest` (`src/modules/schedule/services/requests.ts`) that it carries no past-date precondition, which is why F-07-5's fix names it, but I did not walk an approval as `dev.director`. That is Task 9's ground.
