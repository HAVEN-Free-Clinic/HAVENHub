# Task 4: Applicant journeys (apply, resume draft, onboard)

Walked 2026-07-28 against `localhost:3000` on the `havenhub_uxaudit` database, using the
Task 3 fixtures (`ux-audit-cycle`, `ux.applicant@yale.edu`, `ux.accepted@yale.edu`).

Journey 1 was walked cold, end to end, as a brand-new applicant (`ux.cold@example.com`,
magic-link sign-in) from the signed-out portal through submission and the status tracker.
Journey 2 was walked as `ux.accepted@yale.edu` from the tokenised onboarding link through
submission, the completion screen, and a revisit of the link afterwards. The resume-draft
path was walked last, on the untouched `ux.applicant@yale.edu` fixture, which was left
intact (not submitted).

## What worked well

Recorded so the fixes above do not accidentally undo it.

- The wizard's own Back button preserves every answer, and the review step's per-section
  Edit links return to Review rather than dumping the applicant back into the linear flow.
- Draft autosave on the apply wizard is real and visible ("Saved" under the step heading),
  and every one of the fixture draft's ten answers came back prefilled.
- Conditional questions expand inline with no reload, and department supplement sections
  appear and disappear correctly.
- The review step lists every section with its answers and states what happens after
  submit. It is the strongest screen in either journey.
- The post-submission status tracker (Submitted, In review, Interview, Decision) is clear.
- The magic-link sign-in confirms the address before signing anyone in, and the confirmation
  copy states the 30-minute expiry.

## Needs its own brainstorm

- **F-04-2, draft save for the onboarding contract (L).** Needs a decision on where partial
  contract state lives (a column on `OnboardingContract` versus a separate draft row), what
  happens to a half-filled contract when a director resends or the link expires, and whether
  drawn signature strokes are persisted or deliberately re-collected each time.
- **F-04-18, unifying the two form models (L).** Needs a decision on whether the onboarding
  contract adopts the recruitment wizard shell or both converge on a shared multi-step form
  primitive, and how the contract's block model (sections, agreements, `visibleWhen`) maps
  onto wizard steps without breaking the client/server visibility parity the contract
  already guarantees.

## Coverage notes

Everything the brief asked for was walked. Specifics and gaps:

- **Journey 1 cold path:** walked complete, signed out to submitted, as a brand-new
  applicant via the magic-link path (`ux.cold@example.com`). The Yale SSO path could not be
  walked: Entra is not configured locally ("Entra ID is not configured (AZURE_AD_* unset)"),
  and the dev-login provider requires an existing `Person`, which a cold applicant does not
  have. The magic-link path is a real production path (it is the one the portal offers to
  non-Yale applicants), so this is a gap in coverage of the SSO variant only. Any SSO-only
  behaviour, including how `applicantFirstName` from Entra affects the greeting in F-04-19,
  is unverified.
- **Renewal and transfer applicant types:** not walked. Step 1 offers "Renewing in my
  current department", which is gated on the signed-in identity being an existing member;
  the cold persona is not one, and the accepted persona has no `Person` row either. The
  renewal branch, its department picker, and the transfer-into-current-department guard are
  uncovered.
- **Drawn signatures:** the onboarding pads were completed via "Type instead". Canvas
  drawing was not exercised (no reliable pointer-stroke path through the tooling), so
  stroke capture, the "Clear" button on a drawn signature, and the stored PNG are
  unverified. F-04-17 describes only what is observable in typed mode.
- **Mobile and narrow viewports:** not walked. All observations are at a 1200px-wide
  desktop viewport. The onboarding contract's 4,719px length and the 18-checkbox
  availability list will both be materially worse on a phone, but that is inference, not
  observation, and is deliberately not claimed in any finding.
- **The resume-draft fixture was left intact.** The walk stopped after confirming the
  landing step, the empty progress rail, and full prefill of all ten saved answers; the
  draft was not submitted, so `npm run fixtures:ux` does not need to be re-run.
- **Not attempted, out of scope per the audit brief:** accessibility, contrast, ARIA, and
  design-token drift. One thing was noticed in passing and is recorded here rather than as
  a finding: the three `SHORT_TEXT` agreement fields on step 10 take their entire policy
  body as their accessible name, producing ~250-word accessible names on three text inputs.
  The fix in F-04-16 resolves it as a side effect.
- The only console error seen anywhere in either journey was `[PostHog.js] PostHog was
  initialized without a token`, which is local-environment configuration, not a defect.
