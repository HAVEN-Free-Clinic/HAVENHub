- `ux.fresh@yale.edu` (no phone, no certificate): the `/get-started` gate walked from
  landing through the profile step and the HIPAA step. **Stopped before the training and
  learning steps** so Task 6 keeps a from-zero persona.

  HIPAA panel and a rejected upload.
- `ux.pending@yale.edu` (certificate on file, `verifiedAt` null): the verification-pending
  state. Its certificate was **not** verified.

## What worked well

Recorded so the fixes above do not accidentally undo it.

- Saving the profile step redirects back to the checklist, where the row flips to a green
  "Done" badge with a check and the counter advances. That is a clear, self-evident
  confirmation with no banner needed.
- Every gate step page carries "Back to checklist" and a live "N of 5 complete" readout, so
  the volunteer is never lost inside a step.
- The gate is honest that steps can be done in any order, and it does not force a sequence.
- Completed and pending steps are visually distinct on the checklist (muted background,
  green check) without relying on colour alone.
- The certificate rejection preserved scroll position on `/my-info`, so the error banner was
  visible in the viewport rather than a thousand pixels above the fold.
- The HIPAA panel parses the completion date out of the PDF and shows what it read
  ("Detected completion date: Jun 29, 2026"), which is genuinely more than the volunteer
  could supply themselves.
- The gate has a "Wrong account? Sign out" escape, which matters because dev/SSO sign-in can
  land the wrong identity and the gate otherwise has no navigation.

## Needs its own brainstorm

No `L` findings. The verification wait (F-05-1 through F-05-3) is three small fixes rather
than one large one, but they should be designed together: the state name, the copy, and the
notification all describe the same moment, and shipping any one alone leaves the other two
contradicting it.

## Coverage notes

- **Training and learning steps were deliberately not walked.** Per the task's scope
  boundary, the walk stopped after the profile and HIPAA steps so Task 6 keeps a from-zero
  persona. Neither `/get-started/training` nor `/get-started/learning` was opened in the
  browser at all, to avoid any chance of recording an attempt. Their checklist rows,
  descriptions, CTAs and blocking flags are covered; their step pages are not.
- **The moment the gate lifts was not observed.** No persona could be taken from gated to
  released without completing training and learning. The claim in F-05-5 that there is no
  acknowledgment on release is read from `src/app/get-started/page.tsx:15` (`redirect("/")`,
  no parameter), not observed, and is stated that way in the finding.
- **The true first-time dashboard was not observed.** `ux.fresh` is gated and cannot reach
  it; `dev.volunteer`, used as instructed, has five upcoming shifts, a next shift four days
  out, and completed training. So the empty-state dashboard a genuinely new volunteer sees
  on their first unlocked visit (no shifts, no history) is uncovered, and F-05-9's
  ten-second question is answered against a populated dashboard only.
- **The upload-success feedback was observed on `/get-started/hipaa`, not on `/my-info`.**
  Both routes render the same `HipaaPanel` with the same `certSaved` parameter
  (`src/app/(app)/my-info/page.tsx:101,158` and `src/app/get-started/hipaa/page.tsx:50,65`),
  so the success alert is identical, but the `/my-info` success path was not exercised
  because a second upload would have replaced `dev.volunteer`'s verified certificate with an
  unverified one and disturbed a fixture other tasks rely on. The `/my-info` **rejection**
  path was exercised, which is safe because validation throws before any row is written.
- **Mobile and narrow viewports were not walked.** Everything is at a 1200px desktop
  viewport. The gate's `md:grid-cols-[340px_1fr]` rail and the dashboard's
  `grid-cols-2 sm:grid-cols-4` action row both change shape below `sm`, and F-05-10's
  truncation in particular will behave differently there; that is not claimed either way.
- **Certificate `View` (the in-app PDF viewer) was not opened.** It appears in every
  certificate block observed but was not exercised.
- **Fixture state consumed.** `ux.fresh@yale.edu` is no longer at zero: the profile step is
  complete (`phone` set to 203-555-0142) and one HIPAA certificate is on file, dateless, in
  UNKNOWN_DATE. Its training and learning steps are untouched, so the gate still fires and
  Task 6 still has a gated persona, but Task 6 should not expect a 0-of-5 checklist. The
  fixture guard keys on the person existing, so re-running `npm run fixtures:ux` will not
  restore the zero state; restoring it means clearing `Person.phone` and deleting the
  `HipaaCertificate` row for that person.
- **Fixture state left intact.** `ux.pending@yale.edu`'s certificate was **not** verified and
  is still PENDING_VERIFICATION. `dev.volunteer@yale.edu` gained no certificate row (the
  `.png` upload was rejected at validation) and no other change.
- **Not attempted, out of scope per the audit brief:** accessibility, contrast, ARIA, and
  design-token drift.
- The only console error seen anywhere was `[PostHog.js] PostHog was initialized without a
  token`, which is local-environment configuration, not a defect.
